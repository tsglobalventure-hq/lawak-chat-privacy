LAWAK CHAT - Child Safety Standards

Last updated: January 2026

Developer: TS GLOBAL VENTURE

Package: com.tsglobalventure.lawak_chat

1. Our Commitment

TS GLOBAL VENTURE is committed to protecting children from sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). LAWAK CHAT is rated Teen (13+) and we take child safety seriously.

2. Age Restriction

LAWAK CHAT requires users to be at least 13 years old to create an account. Our app is rated Teen 13+ on Google Play Store.

3. Reporting Mechanism

Users can report child safety concerns through:

  • In-app feedback: Settings → Send Feedback
  • Email: tsglobalventure@gmail.com

All reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

4. Content Moderation

We employ the following measures to prevent child sexual abuse material (CSAM):

  • Room operators (OP/AOP/SOP) can moderate chat rooms
  • Users can be kicked or banned from rooms
  • Message reporting functionality available
  • Content filtering applied to chat messages

5. Compliance with Laws

LAWAK CHAT complies with all applicable child safety laws including:

  • Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission (MCMC) regulations
  • Google Play Child Safety Standards policy
  • Applicable national and regional child protection laws

6. Reporting to Authorities

When we become aware of CSAM or child exploitation, we report to:

  • Relevant national law enforcement authorities
  • National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable
  • Local Malaysian authorities as required by law

7. Contact

For child safety concerns, contact us at:

Email: tsglobalventure@gmail.com
