LAWAK CHAT ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application LAWAK CHAT (the "App").

When you register and use LAWAK CHAT, we may collect:

We use the collected information for:

  • Sending push notifications about messages and app updates
  • Improving app performance and user experience
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, or security issues
